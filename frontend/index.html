Promise

100% Quality Assurance

Every onion bulb is checked for moisture levels under 12%, outer shell thickness, and sorting diameter to ensure export-grade standard consistency.

Ventilated Packaging

We use premium custom leno mesh bags with high ventilation capabilities, ensuring zero decay or sweat damage during long-haul ocean transport.

Hassle-Free Documentation

Full legal compliance under GSTIN and IEC protocols ensures smooth customs clearances and detailed phytosanitary certificate verification.
