Our Agrarian Roots

About Vihaan Agro Overseas

Rooted in Nashik, Maharashtra—the onion capital of India—we deliver top-quality fresh onions, flakes, powder, and seeds directly to global markets.

Our Story

Bridging Indian Farms to Global Tables

Established in Nashik, Maharashtra, Vihaan Agro Overseas is committed to streamlining the agricultural supply chain. We work directly with local farmers in Dindori and Pimpalgaon Baswant to procure premium-quality red onions.

Under the executive management of Gatkal Mangesh Raiaram, we implement standardized harvesting, drying, sorting, and grading methods. From the field to the container, our logistics team monitors freshness and quality factors meticulously.
