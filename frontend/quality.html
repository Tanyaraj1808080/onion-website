Quality Assurance Protocols

Export Quality Standards

We implement rigid sizing, drying, and packaging parameters to ensure compliance with global sanitary and phytosanitary (SPS) regulations.

Quality Specifications

Grades, Sizing, and Sorting Benchmarks

Nashik red onions are globally desired for their pungent taste and thick skin layers. To preserve these qualities across oceans, our grading centers clean and sort the produce based on strict moisture and weight factors.

  • Diameter sizing: Graded in 30-45mm, 45-55mm, and 55mm+ selections.
  • Defect Tolerance: Less than 2% sorting margin for split skins, doubles, or physical damage.
  • Drying Protocol: Sun-cured on farms followed by shadow curing to harden outer skins.

Grading & Calibration

Rotary graders sort onions by size, keeping dimensions uniform.

Moisture Auditing

Rigid auditing keeps moisture levels under 12% to prevent rotting.

Lab Certifications

Batches undergo chemical tests for zero pesticide residues.

Ventilated Packing

Mesh bags preserve oxygenation inside containers.

Compliance & Certifications
