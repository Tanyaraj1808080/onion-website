Shop Onion & Spices

Select from our collection of premium agricultural grade commodities sorted, packaged, and preserved for global marine transport.

🧅 Fresh Red Onions

Premium Nashik red onions featuring multi-layered crimson papery skins, high pungency levels. Size 30-55mm+, moisture < 12%. Sacks packing in 10kg/25kg/50kg mesh.

🥣 Dehydrated Onion Flakes

Processed in hygienic facilities using fresh-slipped pink and red onions. Uniform cuts (10-15mm), moisture-controlled (< 6.5%). Standard 14kg cartons with inner polybags.

🧂 Dehydrated Onion Powder

Milled from dried onions into fine, free-flowing pink-crimson powder. 80-100 mesh grade. Zero artificial dye. Packed in 20kg/25kg Kraft paper bags with poly-liner.

🌱 Onion Seeds

Also known as Nigella seeds, our selected onion seeds are thoroughly cleaned, sifted to remove dust and weed seeds (admixture < 0.5%). Germination rate > 85%.

🚜 Nashik Farm Sourcing

Raw farm-fresh onions harvested directly from local growing blocks in Dindori. Handpicked for premium quality checks and pre-graded for processing.

🏭 Sorting & Packing Base

Hygienic sorting base where onions undergo dry-matter inspection, size grading, and packing. Handled by trained professionals to ensure export standard safety.

🚢 Bulk Container Cargo

Vessel-ready containers loaded carefully for global marine transport. Packed securely in ventilated containers to prevent sweating and decay.

🔍 Sourcing Quality Check

Detailed phytosanitary and batch quality checks to assure compliance with international food industry benchmarks and custom clearances.
